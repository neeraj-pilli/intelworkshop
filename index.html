Demanding Workloads & High AI

Intel Core Ultra Series 3

Codenamed: Panther Lake

Designed for demanding creation, AAA gaming, and intense AI compute workloads with zero performance compromises.

  • Up to 16 Compute Cores (Cougar Cove P-Cores)
  • Intel Xe3 Celestial GPU Architecture
  • Up to 180 Platform TOPS for Heavy AI
  • Ideal for Creators, Gamers & Engineers
Right-Sized AI & Efficient Value

Intel Core Series 3

Codenamed: Wildcat Lake

Brings modern computing and right-sized AI features to budget-conscious, highly mobile laptop buyers.

  • High Efficiency Core Architecture
  • Extended Battery Life Endurance
  • ~40 TOPS NPU for Daily AI Tasks
  • Perfect for Students & Daily Multitasking

Technical Terms Made Simple

Key Differences Explained Simply

Watch each core difference reveal sequentially with simple everyday analogies.

1

Architectural Goal: Peak Power vs. Efficiency

Panther Lake (Ultra Series 3): Designed to push maximal frame rates, render heavy timeline tracks, and run simultaneous complex algorithms.

Wildcat Lake (Series 3): Optimized for sleek mobility, cool operation, and all-day endurance without draining battery life fast.

Student Analogy: Think of Panther Lake as a high-performance sports car, while Wildcat Lake is a super-efficient electric hybrid city car.
2

AI TOPS Engine (Platform TOPS)

Panther Lake: Heavy NPU + Xe3 GPU platform horsepower delivering up to 180 Platform TOPS for local Large Language Models (LLMs) and offline generative AI generation.

Wildcat Lake: Right-sized ~40 TOPS NPU engineered specifically for smart everyday utilities like noise cancellation, background blur, and AI camera tracking.

Student Analogy: Panther Lake is a full film studio VFX engine; Wildcat Lake is a quick automatic filter on your smartphone camera.
3

Graphics Core (Intel Xe3 vs Integrated Xe)

Panther Lake: Houses up to 12 Xe3 Celestial cores that let you run modern 3D games and CAD software smoothly without needing a bulky separate GPU.

Wildcat Lake: Features lightweight integrated Xe graphics geared for multi-display 4K video playback, basic photo editing, and casual gaming.

Student Analogy: Panther Lake brings a built-in gaming console experience; Wildcat Lake provides clear, stutter-free high-def video streaming.
4

Target Pricing & Device Tier

Panther Lake: Found in premium creator workstations, gaming rigs, and high-end ultrabooks starting at ₹90,000+.

Wildcat Lake: Built to bring 18A architecture down to budget-friendly student and everyday laptops between ₹35,000 and ₹70,000.

Student Analogy: Professional workstation gear vs. smart, budget-smart everyday essential tools.
